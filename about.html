About FI Signal Weekly

If you sell to banks and credit unions, timing is everything. A pending merger, a core processing decision, or a new CEO or CIO can open a buying window that closes fast. FI Signal Weekly collects those events in one place, free, and updates weekly.

What's on it

  • Pending mergers — credit union and bank combinations, with vote and close dates when published.
  • Core renewals / conversions — institutions that signed, renewed or are converting core platforms.
  • New executives — CEO and other leadership changes at institutions.

Where the data comes from

Only public sources: NCUA merger notices, FDIC institution data (asset sizes), SEC filings, and company press releases. Every row links to its source. If a fact can't be verified, it isn't listed.

Caveats

Asset sizes are approximate and may be from different dates. This is a research aid, not advice. Always confirm details with the source.
