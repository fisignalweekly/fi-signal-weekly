About FI Signal Weekly

FI Signal Weekly is a free board of buying-window signals at US banks and credit unions. A merger, a core decision, a new digital banking platform or a new executive can open a window to sell. We put those events in one place, each linked to its source.

Who it's for

Fintech account executives, SDRs and sales reps, and the sales and marketing leaders who run their teams.

The board

The board lists verified signals, newest first. New rows are published every weekday, and every row is fact-checked first. Filter by type, state, asset size, bank or credit union, date or keyword, and copy any filtered view as a link.

Signal types on the board:

  • Pending merger: bank and credit union combinations, with vote and close dates when published.
  • Core renewal / conversion: institutions that signed, renewed or are converting a core platform.
  • New executive: CEO and other leadership changes.
  • Digital banking change: an institution that selected or launched a new digital banking platform.
  • Credit unions: CU buys bank, CU merger, CU exec move and CU charter change.

Institution pages

The directory has a page for each of 8,507 US banks and credit unions. Each page shows, where the data exists:

  • Technology stack: online banking (retail and business), mobile app, core and bill pay. Every filled field shows its source and the date we last checked it. Online banking comes from a scan of the login links on the institution's own website. The mobile app has its own row, labeled with its source (an app store listing linked from the institution's website), and never fills online banking. Core and bill pay come only from verified public announcements. Fields we can't verify say "Not yet identified".
  • Assets, deposits and loans: quarterly trends from Q1 2016, compared with a peer median and the median bank or credit union, in dollars or adjusted for inflation. Plus a peer rank by state and asset tier.
  • Strategic priorities: on select institution pages, the priorities the parent company states in its public filings, quoted word for word with the company named, the filing date and a link to the source.
  • Activity: the institution's signals, key executive moves, and merger and charter history.
  • Branch openings: new branches confirmed by the institution's own announcement or its regulator record. These appear on institution pages only, not on the board.
  • Similar institutions: others in the same state and asset range.

Where the data comes from

Public sources only: FDIC and NCUA call reports and records, NCUA merger notices, SEC filings, vendor and institution newsrooms, and news reports. We always cite and link the original publisher. No private or paid data.

How we fact-check

Every row is checked against its source before it's published. If a fact can't be confirmed in a public source, we leave it out. Anything estimated is labeled as an estimate.

Get the signals

  • X: @FISignalWeekly posts selected new signals.

Caveats

Asset sizes come from the latest call reports and may be from different dates. This is a research aid, not advice. Confirm details with the source before you act. Not affiliated with any regulator or vendor.
