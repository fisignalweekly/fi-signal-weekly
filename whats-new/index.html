What's new

Dated notes about what changed on FI Signal Weekly, each with a link to an example page.

November 18, 2026

Institution pages, version 2

A fuller institution page layout is on showcase pages, including Glacier Bank, and is rolling out to all institution pages today. Example: Glacier Bank.

What's new and a public roadmap

A What's new page keeps dated, plain-language release notes, and a public Roadmap page shows what is live, being built, or queued, with estimated dates. Example: Roadmap.

Vendor platform change feed

A new Vendor platform changes page collects public notices about digital banking platforms and lists institutions that may be affected, based on a login-page scan. The first entry is Jack Henry's planned retirement of NetTeller Online Banking and Cash Management on July 1, 2027. Example: Vendor platform changes.

Territory view

A new Territory page lists banks with assets strictly above $1.4 billion and an Eastern Time headquarters, grouped by HQ city, with the rule published on the page. The same view is a URL filter on the board and in the directory. Example: Territory view.

Provider fields on institution pages

Every institution page now has a Technology providers section. Digital banking comes from a login-page scan or a verified announcement; core is listed only when we have a verified public source. Example: Ashland.
