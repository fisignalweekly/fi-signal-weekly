How the comparison works

“vs peers” indexes each line to 100 at Q4 2020. Peer median: the middle of today's under $500M banks (the same tier as the peer rank below) that reported in Q4 2020, this one left out, each indexed to 100 there. Median bank: the middle of all FDIC-insured banks that reported in both Q4 2020 and that quarter. All banks, total: the sum of every filer's assets each quarter, so it includes banks that opened, merged or closed. Peer and median lines count only institutions that still file today, so they leave out ones that merged away or closed. Inflation uses the BLS CPI-U (all items, U.S. city average), averaged by quarter; the dollar view is in Q2 2026 dollars. The line above compares assets in Q2 2026 with Q4 2020.
