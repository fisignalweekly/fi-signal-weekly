How the comparison works

“vs peers” indexes each line to 100 at Q1 2016. Peer median: the middle of today's under $500M credit unions (the same tier as the peer rank below) that reported in Q1 2016, this one left out, each indexed to 100 there. Median credit union: the middle of all federally insured credit unions that reported in both Q1 2016 and that quarter. All credit unions, total: the sum of every filer's assets each quarter, so it includes credit unions that opened, merged or closed. Peer and median lines count only institutions that still file today, so they leave out ones that merged away or closed. Inflation uses the BLS CPI-U (all items, U.S. city average), averaged by quarter; the dollar view is in Q2 2026 dollars. The line above compares assets in Q2 2026 with Q1 2016. Source: NCUA 5300 call reports and BLS.
