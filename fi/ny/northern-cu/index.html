How the comparison works

“vs peers” indexes each line to 100 at Q2 2026. Peer median: the middle of today's $500M–$1B credit unions (the same tier as the peer rank below) that reported in Q2 2026, this one left out, each indexed to 100 there. Median credit union: the middle of all federally insured credit unions that reported in both Q2 2026 and that quarter. All credit unions, total: the sum of every filer's assets each quarter, so it includes credit unions that opened, merged or closed. Peer and median lines count only institutions that still file today, so they leave out ones that merged away or closed. Inflation uses the BLS CPI-U (all items, U.S. city average), averaged by quarter; the dollar view is in Q2 2026 dollars. The line above compares assets in Q2 2026 with Q2 2026.
