Eastern Time banks above $1.4 billion

339 banks. A static territory test: banks only, assets strictly above $1.4 billion, Eastern Time by headquarters city.

The rule we used

Credit unions are not on this list. Asset sizes come from the FDIC call report (2026 Q2). None of the banks in the file have assets of exactly $1.4 billion, so “above $1.4 billion” and a floor of $1,400,000,000 are the same set today.

Eastern Time is based on the bank’s headquarters city, not its branch network.

  • Entirely Eastern Time: Connecticut, Delaware, Georgia, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, and the District of Columbia.
  • Florida, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, and Tennessee are split. For those states we use the bank’s FDIC headquarters county, as follows.
    • Florida: Eastern, except the western panhandle counties of Bay, Calhoun, Escambia, Holmes, Jackson, Okaloosa, Santa Rosa, Walton, and Washington (Central). No bank on this list is headquartered in Gulf County, which is split.
    • Indiana: Eastern, except Gibson, Jasper, Lake, LaPorte, Newton, Perry, Porter, Posey, Spencer, Starke, Vanderburgh, and Warrick counties (Central), per 49 CFR 71.5. The city of Jasper is in Dubois County and is Eastern; Jasper County is Central.
    • Kentucky: Eastern, except these 41 western counties, which are Central: Adair, Allen, Ballard, Barren, Breckinridge, Butler, Caldwell, Calloway, Carlisle, Christian, Clinton, Crittenden, Cumberland, Daviess, Edmonson, Fulton, Graves, Grayson, Green, Hancock, Hart, Henderson, Hickman, Hopkins, Livingston, Logan, Lyon, Marshall, McCracken, McLean, Metcalfe, Monroe, Muhlenberg, Ohio, Russell, Simpson, Todd, Trigg, Union, Warren, and Webster.
    • Michigan: Eastern, except Dickinson, Gogebic, Iron, and Menominee counties in the western Upper Peninsula (Central).
    • Tennessee: Eastern only in these 30 counties: Anderson, Blount, Bradley, Campbell, Carter, Claiborne, Cocke, Grainger, Greene, Hamblen, Hamilton, Hancock, Hawkins, Jefferson, Johnson, Knox, Loudon, McMinn, Meigs, Monroe, Morgan, Polk, Rhea, Roane, Scott, Sevier, Sullivan, Unicoi, Union, and Washington. Nashville, Memphis, and the rest of the state are Central.
  • Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands use Atlantic Time and are not on this list.

The digital provider shown on each row is the current retail value from our login-page scan, when we have one. It is not a core-system field. “Not yet identified” means we have not published a current retail provider for that bank.

The same filter works as a URL on the board and in the directory: ?charter=bank&tz=et&assets_min=1400000000.
